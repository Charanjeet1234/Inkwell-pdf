Security

How Inkwell PDF Studio protects you and your documents.

Your documents stay on your device

Inkwell opens, edits, signs and converts files entirely inside your web browser. Documents are never uploaded to our servers, so there is no copy of your files for anyone to leak or misuse. Close the tab and they are gone from Inkwell.

Protections built into the website

  • HTTPS everywhere with HTTP Strict Transport Security, so connections cannot be downgraded.
  • A strict Content Security Policy that only allows code from Inkwell itself and one pinned library host (jsDelivr).
  • Integrity-checked libraries: each third-party library is pinned to an exact version with a cryptographic hash. If a file were ever altered, your browser would refuse to run it.
  • No embedding: other sites cannot frame Inkwell to trick you into clicking.
  • No camera, microphone or location access, and no advertising or tracking cookies.
  • Converted Word documents are cleaned of scripts and unsafe links before they are shown.

Accounts

  • Sign-in uses one-time email codes or Google sign-in, so there is no Inkwell password to steal or reuse.
  • Accounts are run by Supabase. Your sign-in session is stored only in your browser and is refreshed automatically.
  • Your account holds your email address, not your documents.

Password-protected PDFs

Inkwell encrypts PDFs with AES-256. Passwords are used only in your browser and are never sent anywhere. Inkwell cannot recover a forgotten password, and it does not remove protection from a PDF unless you enter its password.

Reporting a security issue

If you believe you have found a security problem, email [support email]. Please give us a reasonable time to fix it before sharing details publicly. We do not take legal action against good-faith research.
