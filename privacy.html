Privacy Policy

Last updated [date]

This policy explains what [Company legal name] ("we") collects when you use Inkwell PDF Studio.

Your documents never leave your device

PDFs, Word files, images, signatures and everything you type are processed inside your browser. We do not upload, store or see them.

Stored on your device

Your browser keeps a few settings locally: theme, saved signatures and templates (only if you choose to save them), and your sign-in session. You can clear them at any time by clearing this site's data in your browser.

Your free account

To save or download files you create a free account. Accounts are handled by Supabase. We store your email address, when you signed up and last signed in, and whether you asked for emails about new features. If you sign in with Google, we also receive the name and profile picture on your Google account. We use this to count our users, keep your account secure and, only if you opted in, to email you about Inkwell. We never sell it.

You can sign out at any time, and ask us to delete your account by emailing [support email].

Anonymous usage statistics

The Inkwell website counts visits with Vercel Web Analytics, without cookies. It records things like the country, browser and device type, and which features are used (for example "saved a PDF" or "created an account"), never file names, file contents or your email address.

Third-party services

Inkwell loads its software libraries and fonts from content delivery networks (jsDelivr and Google Fonts). As with any website, these services receive your IP address and browser details when the files are downloaded.

Your rights

You can ask us for a copy of the personal data we hold about you, or ask us to correct or delete it, by contacting [support email]. We keep payment records for as long as the law requires.
